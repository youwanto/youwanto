# Product vision

YouWanto helps visitors discover fashion, beauty and lifestyle products, select an appropriate variant and continue to a partner retailer. The current inspected inventory includes 24S links. Additional feed sources are represented in the schema and design documents; their presence does not establish that scheduled production ingestion exists.

## Current boundaries

Product browsing, collection filtering, product details, credentials sign-in and cart storage exist. Payment capture, order creation, fulfillment and a functioning checkout are not implemented in this repository. Partner checkout is external. Avoid presenting shipping/returns claims as YouWanto guarantees.

## Priorities

1. Make prices, availability, filters and destination links reliable.
2. Make products easy to find through navigation and search.
3. Explain the partner-store relationship before the outbound click.
4. Add reasons to return: saved items, price history, restock alerts.
5. Expand merchants only with repeatable import validation and monitoring.

## Measurement plan

Track product impressions, product opens, no-result filters/searches, variant selection and outbound retailer clicks. Add saved-item and alert conversion metrics when those features exist. These are proposed events; analytics is not claimed implemented.

Use outbound click-through rate and successful discovery as initial measures. Revenue attribution requires confirmed affiliate tracking; a raw outbound click is not a purchase.
